Design story

Balanced contrasts define the Aeron silhouette.

We started with a generous chamfered tabletop that invites touch, then floated it on a tensioned frame of brushed brass. The resulting negative space keeps the generous scale feeling light and sculptural.

Walnut with ceramic fusion

A kiln-fired ceramic skin protects the walnut substrate without masking its character. The finish withstands red wine, citrus, and 500 °F serveware.

Invisible leaf mechanism

The extension leaf nests within the base and glides out silently on magnetic rails, maintaining the uninterrupted waterfall grain.

Tailored to your space

Choose among three stain palettes and optional stone inlays. Each table is custom-built in our Seattle studio after a detailed consultation.
